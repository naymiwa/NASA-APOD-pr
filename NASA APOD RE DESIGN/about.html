About

NASA APOD is a gallery website that displays the Astronomy Picture of the Day from NASA's official API. Every day, NASA publishes a different image or photograph of our universe, along with a brief explanation written by a professional astronomer.

This website is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It fetches the data directly from the NASA Open APIs and shows the picture, the date, the explanation, and the image credit.
